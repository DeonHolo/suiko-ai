# Editor wording feedback — 14 November 2026

Original discussion note; the owner subsequently approved removing Decision 1 from the main flow and using **Source text retrieved**. The implemented scope and rationale are recorded in [the decision](../../docs/30-decision-1-retirement.md). Other questions below remain discussion items.

## Requested revision to preserve

- Replace the ambiguous source status badge **Read** with clearer wording. The owner suggested **Checked source** or similar. Discuss the final label before implementation: this status means source text was retrieved, not that its accuracy or trustworthiness was verified. **Source text retrieved** is a candidate that preserves that distinction.
- Consider the matching timestamp wording (**Read [time]**) when revising the badge, so the ambiguity is not left immediately below it.

## Questions and concerns raised for discussion

- The optional second source check does not explain that it is another AI, what it checks, what the result looks like, or whether greater accuracy is established. The wording “second opinion” could suggest a human professional.
- Explain the claim/source labels and whether other statuses exist.
- Explain the difference between the section heading **Needs attention** and the individual result **Needs confirmation**.
- Explain **Overview** and why its generated text can be withheld when its supporting references cannot be checked against the saved draft.

Keep these as discussion items until the owner agrees to further revisions. Do not silently treat a successful retrieval as source verification or claim the second model is generally more accurate.
