# AI-02 · Agree the shared review example and rules

**Status:** in-progress
**Owner:** You, backend/frontend teammates, and the policy scenario owner
**Blocked by:** None

The 12 November interview settled the first-build workflow and Deon's AI panel ownership. Use those decisions and the local prototype to prepare one example that the panel and backend can both build from.

## Outcome

The team can recognize the same story, submitted version, active rules, and review result in a sample request, an app record, and the AI panel.

## Steps

- [ ] Share a first draft and a revised copy with optional source notes. Include story and version IDs, the previous submission when available, and the active rule versions.
- [ ] Share the expected overview, rule-linked findings with passages, comparison, metadata suggestions, and a failed review state. Agree the fields and actions used to start a review, retrieve a result, and save an editor's metadata choice.
- [ ] Agree the starting wording for a shared sourcing rule and a conditional outside-asset rule. Identify the designated editor for the demo.
- [ ] Record the selected input limits, tag and section choices, contract version, and PDI actions with both teammates.

## Done when

The AI panel and backend teammates can each point to the same sample inputs, outputs, and workflow actions. The demo rule wording and remaining platform questions are recorded.

## Evidence and current state
