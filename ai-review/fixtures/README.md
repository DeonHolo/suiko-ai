# Media production test cases

These two fictional cases use the same plain-text request format as the library story. Each case gives Gemini two versions, a checklist, and allowed labels. Compare its answer with the expectations below after the test.

| Case | Changes to look for | Checklist and suggested details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [River cleanup video package](river-video.request.json) | The cleanup moved from Saturday at 8 a.m. to Sunday at 9 a.m. Report it once with quotes from the anchor line and the text shown on screen. Staff footage was replaced with an outside drone clip. | Ask the producer to confirm permission for the drone clip. The new script and on-screen time agree. Suggest Local news, Environment, and Video package. Keep the clip out of the description. |
| [Harbor Film Night social teaser](sponsored-caption.request.json) | The public caption lost the paid partnership disclosure for Lantern Coffee. The full video moved from Friday to Saturday at 6 p.m. | Flag the missing disclosure using the caption and paid production note. The caption has no unsupported superlatives. Suggest Arts and culture, Film, and Social video. Build the short description from the teaser title and release card. |

The rules are examples for this project. The producer or editor checks the footage permission or restores the disclosure before the piece goes out.
